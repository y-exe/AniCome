AniCome overlays Niconico comments on Android video apps and synchronizes them with playback.

Features:
• Automatic series and episode matching
• Official Niconico, d Anime, live archive and Jikkyo comment sources
• Per-app comment position, size, opacity and speed
• Advance comment downloads, automatic cache cleanup and offline comment playback
• NG-word filtering and a compact floating playback panel

Notification access is used to detect playback information. Display-over-other-apps permission is required to overlay comments. Niconico login is required during setup. Requests for comments and series information are sent to third-party services.

F-Droid builds receive application updates through F-Droid. Artwork and preview comments are fetched at runtime rather than bundled. A network connection is required for the initial download.

AniCome does not play or download the streaming services' videos. It is an independent application, not an official product of Niconico or any supported streaming service.
