<b>Your usual anime, with a little company.</b>

Share the excitement of a favorite scene or laugh at an unexpected comment. AniCome brings Niconico's scrolling comments to your Android streaming apps, adding the feeling of watching together to the way you already watch.

<b>Keep watching in your favorite app</b>
AniCome matches the series, episode and playback position to find and synchronize comments. Sources include official Niconico videos, d Anime videos, live archives and Jikkyo logs. You can also search manually and fine-tune the timing.

<b>Make yourself comfortable</b>
Adjust comment position, text size, opacity and speed for each streaming app. Filter unwanted words and use the compact floating panel to adjust comments and timing while you watch.

<b>Get comments ready ahead of time</b>
Download comments by series and episode to use them offline later. Set an expiry time for automatically cached comments to keep storage from filling up.

<b>Supported apps</b>
<ul>
<li>Standard playback information: Netflix, U-NEXT, ABEMA, DMM TV, TVer and NHK ONE</li>
<li>Accessibility-assisted detection: Prime Video, d Anime Store, Bandai Channel and Hulu</li>
</ul>

Accessibility-assisted apps use titles or playback times shown on screen. Permission is explained when you first select one of these apps. For screen-based timing, show the player controls after seeking to resynchronize. Automatic matching and synchronization may not work for every title or app state.

<b>Getting started</b>
Niconico sign-in is required during setup. Notification access detects playback information, and display-over-other-apps permission lets comments appear over your video. Fetching comments, series information, artwork and preview comments connects to third-party services. F-Droid builds receive updates through F-Droid.

AniCome is an independent application, not an official product of Niconico or any streaming service. It does not play or download the streaming services' videos.
